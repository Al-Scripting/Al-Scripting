I am an MSc student in Computer Science (Software Design) at **Ontario Tech University**, and a Graduate
Research Assistant at the **[Code & Sorcery Lab](https://cslab.quest/)**. My bachelor's degree is a
BIT in Networking and IT Security, with Dean's List and the CCNA.

Networking and security by training. Software design and AI by research. I am not a game developer.
I am a software designer who uses games as a tool to test new ideas, from reinforcement learning
agents to LLM characters that remember and stay in character.
